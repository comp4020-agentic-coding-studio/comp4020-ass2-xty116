## The question

An image sonification must decide where scanning begins, how long it takes and
which pixels deserve sound. We compare row, column and radial paths while
keeping a tactile or tabular alternative available.

## Signal chain

Image pixels → choose scan path → map horizontal position to stereo, energy
band to timbre and brightness to level → announce scan progress. The route
through the image constructs time that the original image did not have.

Chandra's [Deep Field sonification](https://chandra.si.edu/sound/cdf.html)
documents how colour, brightness and horizontal position are assigned to
auditory features.
