## The question

A dynamic spectrum contains time, frequency and intensity. It also contains
missing channels, terrestrial interference and calibration artefacts. We test
scan paths that preserve two dimensions instead of flattening everything into
one melody.

## Signal chain

Frequency-by-time intensity matrix → mark unavailable cells → select a scan
direction → map frequency to pitch and intensity to loudness → keep flagged
noise audible or explicitly muted. Silence is also an editorial decision.

Students annotate which features are observations, which are suspected noise
and which cannot be classified from the data alone.
