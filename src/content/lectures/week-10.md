## The question

Sound can expand access for blind and low-vision listeners, yet exclude Deaf
and hard-of-hearing people, sensory-sensitive users or anyone in a quiet
environment. We design coordinated audio, text, table and visual channels
without calling one a substitute for every other.

## Signal chain

One dataset → equivalent inspectable encodings → keyboard-operable transport →
user-controlled level and speed → structured description. The scientific claim
must survive when audio is unavailable.

Chandra's [A Universe of Sound](https://chandra.si.edu/sound/) provides
documented examples and explains its collaboration with blind and low-vision
participants.
