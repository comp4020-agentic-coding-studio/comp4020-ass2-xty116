## The question

The famous chirp is grounded in detector strain, but published audio may be
filtered, amplified or shifted. We compare time-domain strain, a spectrogram
and playback while naming each operation.

## Signal chain

Interferometer strain → calibrate and band-pass → isolate a short event window
→ optionally shift or amplify → render to audio. The rising frequency is in
the signal; the playback level and audible placement are presentation choices.

Use the [LIGO Scientific Collaboration's GW150914 page](https://ligo.org/detections/gw150914/)
for the event record and data-release links. The lecture deck walks through the
chain before the lab comparison.
