## The question

Pulsars produce regular radio pulses, but the recording available to us is
already sampled, filtered and often folded over many rotations. We inspect a
pulse profile before deciding whether to audify amplitude, trigger an event or
compress time.

## Signal chain

Radio intensity over time → remove baseline → identify period → fold repeated
pulses → map amplitude to loudness. Tempo grids are not part of the source
unless the designer adds them.

The [ATNF Pulsar Catalogue](https://www.atnf.csiro.au/research/pulsar/psrcat/)
provides published pulsar parameters and makes a useful provenance starting
point for the first signal study.
