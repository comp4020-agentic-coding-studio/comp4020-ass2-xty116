## The question

Quantisation can invent steps, reverberation can imply scale, a major chord can
suggest resolution and selective filtering can erase ambiguity. We audit two
versions of one dataset and label the rhetorical work done by musical choices.

## Signal chain

Source values → list transformations → identify added musical structure →
compare interpretation with and without a mapping legend → revise. A beautiful
result is welcome, but beauty is not evidence.

The class develops a disclosure checklist covering provenance, units, scaling,
filtering, compression, missing data, uncertainty and compositional additions.
