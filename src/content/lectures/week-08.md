## The question

Spectra already resemble timelines, but the horizontal axis is wavelength, not
time. Playing left to right creates a temporal sequence. We use a reference
tone and spoken axis markers so listeners can compare line position without
assuming that consonance carries scientific meaning.

## Signal chain

Wavelength and flux pairs → preserve or transform wavelength spacing → map flux
to level → add axis landmarks → compare shifted copies. Any logarithmic
transform is stated in the legend.

The lab evaluates whether a listener can identify a known shift, not whether
the spectrum produces an attractive scale.
