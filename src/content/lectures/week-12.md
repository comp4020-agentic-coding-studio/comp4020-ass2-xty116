## The question

The final lecture becomes a listening room. Each instrument offers a short
guided encounter, source data, a non-audio representation and a complete
mapping disclosure. Visitors respond to one scientific question rather than
rating musical taste.

## Signal chain

Public dataset → documented cleaning → inspectable mapping → accessible
interface → listening task → evidence from visitors → revision note. The
instrument succeeds when its claim and its limitations stand without the
designer beside it.

We close by asking what sonification adds to scientific inquiry and where a
graph, table or spoken description remains the more honest tool.
