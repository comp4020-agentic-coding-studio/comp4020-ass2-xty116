## The brief

> Make the regularity of one pulsar inspectable without quietly turning the
> signal into a musical beat.

Analyse the timing, produce two contrasting renderings and explain what folding,
filtering and time compression change. You are marked on the argument carried
by the pair, not on production polish.

## What you submit

A web page or PDF study, two playable audio files, the processed data table and
a methods file sufficient for another student to reproduce the mapping.
