## The brief

> Build a vocabulary for hearing data by making four small mappings and
> recording what each one reveals, hides and suggests.

Use the weekly experiments from weeks 1–4. At least one entry must compare two
mappings of identical values; at least one must document a failed mapping.

## What you submit

One accessible document or web page containing four entries, mapping legends,
source links and a short index. Embed audio or link stable files, but ensure the
scientific comparison is possible from the included tables and descriptions.
