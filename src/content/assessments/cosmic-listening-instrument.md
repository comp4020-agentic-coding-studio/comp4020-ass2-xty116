## The brief

> Build a public listening instrument that helps a person investigate one
> precise astronomical question while exposing every design choice between
> data and sound.

Use a citable dataset small enough to understand deeply. The instrument may be
beautiful, playful or musical, but it must never imply that its arrangement is
an unprocessed recording. Scope one primary interaction and make it excellent.

## What you submit

Submit a public URL, source repository, a 500-word methods and disclosure page,
the source or retrieval instructions for the data, and a testing record from at
least five first-time users. Include a two-minute screen recording only as
backup; markers assess the live instrument.
