## The brief

> Treat one public sonification as an argument: reconstruct how it works, test
> what it lets a listener know, and redesign one consequential choice.

Choose a work with enough documentation to identify its source and mapping.
Your critique must address uncertainty, attribution and at least two different
access needs. Taste alone is not evidence.

## What you submit

An accessible document with a signal-chain diagram, cited listening
observations, mapping table and a small redesign sketch or prototype. Include
alt text or a structured description for every figure.
