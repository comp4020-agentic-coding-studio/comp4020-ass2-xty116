## Before the lab

Bring a 12-value scalar sequence and a one-sentence listening task.

## In the lab

Build three minimal mappings. Rotate listeners without explaining the answer,
record what they detect, then compare accuracy and confidence.

## Leave with

A mapping matrix that records range, scale, timing, test result and the rule
chosen for the notebook.
