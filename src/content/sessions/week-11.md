## Before the lab

Prepare a complete mapping legend and a version of the instrument that a peer
can run without an explanation.

## In the lab

Conduct a hostile audit: infer what the interface seems to claim, then compare
that impression with its source and methods. Revise the largest mismatch.

## Leave with

A signed disclosure checklist, before-and-after evidence and a precise final
testing question.
