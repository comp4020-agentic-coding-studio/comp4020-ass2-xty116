## Before the lab

Select a pulsar record from the provided ATNF shortlist and read its period.

## In the lab

Mark pulse arrivals, fold the series by the published period and compare the
aggregate profile with the original timing. Discuss what repeated averaging
removes.

## Leave with

A labelled plot, short audio rendering and methods note ready for the Pulsar
Signal Study.
