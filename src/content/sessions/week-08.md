## Before the lab

Bring two small spectra that share at least one identifiable line.

## In the lab

Create alternating and simultaneous versions. Add an axis landmark, test line
identification with peers and record where musical interval expectations cause
confusion.

## Leave with

A 60-second comparison, accessible transcript and evidence for the mapping you
will critique.
