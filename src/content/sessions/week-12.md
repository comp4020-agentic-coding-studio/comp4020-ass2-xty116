## Before the lab

Deploy the instrument, prepare headphones and print a QR-free fallback URL plus
a one-page mapping card.

## In the lab

Visitors use the instrument before hearing its designer speak. Record whether
they identify the intended pattern, where controls fail and what they believe
the sound represents.

## Leave with

A working public instrument, anonymised observations and one evidence-led
revision note submitted with the capstone.
