## Before the lab

Bring the final Sonification Critique and identify one uncertainty its example
handles poorly.

## In the lab

Apply three uncertainty strategies to one curve. Ask listeners to identify the
least certain region and decide which cue interferes least with the primary
pattern.

## Leave with

A prototype, raw table and test note that becomes the uncertainty plan for the
capstone instrument.
