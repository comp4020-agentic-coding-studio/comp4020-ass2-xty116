## Before the lab

Complete the access checklist for your capstone prototype before asking someone
else to test it.

## In the lab

Rotate through no-audio, no-visual and keyboard-only passes. Test the same
scientific question in each mode and document where equivalence breaks.

## Leave with

An access audit containing observed failures, prioritised revisions and the
alternative representation that will ship.
