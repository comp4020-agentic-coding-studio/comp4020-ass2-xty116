# Accessibility beyond the graph

<p className="lede">Sound adds a route to scientific meaning. It does not replace every other route.</p>

---

## Access is a system, not a format

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="metric gold"><strong>hear</strong><span>pattern, event and comparison</span></div>
  <div className="metric"><strong>see</strong><span>shape, position and overview</span></div>
  <div className="metric warm"><strong>read</strong><span>values, units and explanation</span></div>
</div>

Sonification can support blind and low-vision learners while excluding others if audio is the only route.

---

{/* _class: compact */}

## One question, coordinated channels

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Channel</th><th>Representation</th><th>Task</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Audio</td><td>pitch contour</td><td>hear the central dip</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Visual</td><td>labelled line plot</td><td>inspect shape and position</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Table</td><td>sample, value, unit, flag</td><td>retrieve exact values</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Description</td><td>pattern + limits in prose</td><td>understand the conclusion</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

The scientific conclusion should survive when any one channel is unavailable.

---

## Equivalent does not mean identical

<div className="deck-grid">
  <div className="choice warm"><p className="panel-title">Mechanical substitution</p><p>A transcript lists note names but never explains the data trend.</p><span>Format changed; task support did not.</span></div>
  <div className="choice gold"><p className="panel-title">Task equivalence</p><p>A structured description names the dip, its location, size and uncertainty.</p><span>The same question can be answered.</span></div>
</div>

Choose an alternative by the work it enables.

---

## Control belongs to the listener

<div className="process-flow">
  <div><strong>Start</strong><span>No autoplay; clear context first.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Adjust</strong><span>Level, speed and layer controls.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Stop</strong><span>Immediate keyboard-operable transport.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Return</strong><span>Replay regions without losing place.</span></div>
</div>

Control is part of meaning because timing and repetition shape interpretation.

---

## A keyboard-only pass

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Action</th><th>Required feedback</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Tab to transport</td><td>visible focus and unambiguous label</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Activate play</td><td>status changes without relying on colour</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Switch mapping</td><td>new rule is announced and displayed</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Stop</td><td>audio ends immediately; focus remains stable</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

If the interface works only with a mouse, the scientific instrument is incomplete.

---

## Design for listening differences

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="panel gold"><p className="panel-title">Hearing range</p><p>Keep essential cues out of extreme pitch regions.</p></div>
  <div className="panel"><p className="panel-title">Sensory load</p><p>Allow layers, motion and repetition to be reduced.</p></div>
  <div className="panel warm"><p className="panel-title">Equipment</p><p>Do not require stereo or expensive headphones.</p></div>
</div>

Accessibility is not a single persona. Offer adjustable, redundant routes.

---

## Test by removing a channel

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="choice gold"><p className="panel-title">Audio only</p><p>Can the trend and uncertainty be identified?</p></div>
  <div className="choice"><p className="panel-title">Visual only</p><p>Can playback state and mapping be understood?</p></div>
  <div className="choice warm"><p className="panel-title">Text + keyboard</p><p>Can the same conclusion be reached and controlled?</p></div>
</div>

Record task success, not simply whether a feature technically exists.
