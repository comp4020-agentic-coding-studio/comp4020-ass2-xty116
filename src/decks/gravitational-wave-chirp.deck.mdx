# The gravitational-wave chirp

<p className="lede">The rising pattern belongs to the event. The playback volume, filtering and pitch range belong to the rendering.</p>

---

  <div className="metric gold"><strong>2015</strong><span>GW150914 observed on 14 September</span></div>

<p className="source-note">Source: LIGO GW150914 event record</p>

---

## From arm length to event window

<div className="process-flow">
  <div><strong>Interfere light</strong><span>Compare two perpendicular arms.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Calibrate</strong><span>Convert detector response to strain.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Compare sites</strong><span>Check time and signal consistency.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Select window</strong><span>Place the event beside nearby noise.</span></div>
</div>

The signal is extremely small and always encountered inside detector noise.

---

## Three renderings, three claims

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Rendering</th><th>Operation</th><th>Consequence</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Calibrated window</td><td>direct playback path</td><td>closest to timing, difficult to hear</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Filtered + amplified</td><td>reduce selected noise bands</td><td>pattern clearer, context altered</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Frequency shifted</td><td>move spectrum upward</td><td>audible detail, measured band changed</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

Clarity and fidelity are related, not identical.

---

## Two detectors are part of the evidence

<div className="deck-grid">
  <div className="panel gold"><p className="panel-title">Hanford</p><p>One strain series, local detector noise and quality state.</p></div>

Hearing only a polished combined chirp removes the cross-site comparison that helped support the detection.
