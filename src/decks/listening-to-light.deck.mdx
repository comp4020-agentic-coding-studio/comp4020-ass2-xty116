# Listening to light

## An image has no beginning

SLOP3255 · Week 7

---

{/* _class: dense */}

## Sonification creates time

An image presents many positions at once.

Audio unfolds.

To hear an image, we choose:

- an origin
- a route
- a speed
- a rule for overlapping sources

That traversal becomes part of the interpretation.

---

## Three scan geometries

**Rows:** predictable, preserves horizontal order  
**Columns:** foregrounds vertical structure  
**Radial:** suits objects organised around a centre

No route is neutral. A route should match the question asked of the image.

---

{/* _class: compact */}

## Keep dimensions independent

Horizontal position → stereo position

Vertical position → pitch

Brightness → level

Energy band → timbre

If two data dimensions share one sound dimension, their meanings become hard to
separate.

---

{/* _class: compact */}

## Tell the listener where they are

Use scan-progress cues, axis announcements or landmarks.

Provide:

- a structured image description
- source layers and units
- a way to replay a region
- independent layer controls

Orientation is part of access.
