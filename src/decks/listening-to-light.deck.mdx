# Listening to light

<p className="lede">An image presents many positions at once. Audio forces us to choose a beginning, route and speed.</p>

---

## Sonification creates time

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="metric gold"><strong>2D</strong><span>image positions coexist</span></div>
  <div className="metric"><strong>1D</strong><span>audio unfolds in sequence</span></div>
  <div className="metric warm"><strong>route</strong><span>the bridge is a design decision</span></div>
</div>

Traversal becomes part of the interpretation, even when every pixel value is preserved.

---

## Three scan geometries

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="panel gold"><p className="panel-title">Rows</p><p>Predictable. Preserves horizontal order and raster logic.</p></div>
  <div className="panel"><p className="panel-title">Columns</p><p>Foregrounds vertical structure and height changes.</p></div>
  <div className="panel warm"><p className="panel-title">Radial</p><p>Suits objects organised around a chosen centre.</p></div>
</div>

No route is neutral. Choose the route that supports the image question.

---

## Keep dimensions independent

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Image dimension</th><th>Sound dimension</th><th>Listener task</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>horizontal position</td><td>stereo position</td><td>locate left / right</td></tr>
    <tr><td>vertical position</td><td>pitch</td><td>estimate height</td></tr>
    <tr><td>brightness</td><td>level</td><td>compare intensity</td></tr>
    <tr><td>energy band</td><td>timbre</td><td>separate layers</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

If two data dimensions share one sound dimension, their meanings become difficult to separate.

---

## Layers need controls

<div className="deck-grid">
  <div className="panel gold"><p className="panel-title">Scientific layers</p><ul><li>energy bands</li><li>source catalogue</li><li>exposure map</li><li>background estimate</li></ul></div>
  <div className="panel"><p className="panel-title">Listener controls</p><ul><li>solo / mute</li><li>replay region</li><li>speed</li><li>scan direction</li></ul></div>
</div>

An all-at-once mix can be impressive and analytically useless.

---

## Tell the listener where they are

<div className="process-flow">
  <div><strong>Announce</strong><span>State origin, image size and scan route.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Landmark</strong><span>Mark axes, centre and known sources.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Progress</strong><span>Expose position during playback.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Return</strong><span>Allow region replay and layer inspection.</span></div>
</div>

Orientation is part of access, not an optional narration layer.
