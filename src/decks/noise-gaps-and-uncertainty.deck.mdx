# Noise, gaps and uncertainty

<p className="lede">Clean sound can overstate certainty. An honest instrument preserves the limits of its evidence.</p>

---

## A value is not alone

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="metric gold"><strong>0.83</strong><span>reported measurement</span></div>
  <div className="metric"><strong>±0.04</strong><span>estimated uncertainty</span></div>
  <div className="metric warm"><strong>Q2</strong><span>quality flag and observing context</span></div>
</div>

Rendering only 0.83 removes the information needed to judge how much confidence it deserves.

---

## Missing is not zero

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>State</th><th>Meaning</th><th>Auditory treatment</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>0</td><td>a valid measured value</td><td>render using the normal rule</td></tr>
    <tr><td>NA</td><td>no valid value exists</td><td>explicit gap marker</td></tr>
    <tr><td>flagged</td><td>value exists but is suspect</td><td>retain value + quality cue</td></tr>
    <tr><td>below limit</td><td>bounded non-detection</td><td>threshold cue + limit value</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

Silence alone cannot distinguish these four states.

## Four uncertainty strategies

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Strategy</th><th>Example</th><th>Best for</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Boundary</td><td>low and high tones around estimate</td><td>reading an interval</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Texture</td><td>more noise as confidence falls</td><td>continuous quality overview</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Repetition</td><td>multiple samples from a distribution</td><td>variation and overlap</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Layer</td><td>separate optional quality channel</td><td>expert inspection</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

No strategy is inherently honest. Test whether the cue supports the intended uncertainty task.

---

## Avoid the “bad data” instrument

<div className="deck-grid">
  <div className="choice warm"><p className="panel-title">Punitive cue</p><p>Harsh distortion marks every uncertain value.</p><span>Uncertainty begins to sound like failure.</span></div>
  <div className="choice gold"><p className="panel-title">Informative cue</p><p>A bounded, optional texture shows confidence without replacing the value.</p><span>Quality remains data, not judgement.</span></div>
</div>

The emotional character of a cue affects interpretation.

---

## Test the uncertainty task

<div className="process-flow">
  <div><strong>Locate</strong><span>Which samples are least certain?</span></div>
  <div><strong>Compare</strong><span>Do two intervals overlap?</span></div>
  <div><strong>Detect</strong><span>Where are values missing?</span></div>
  <div><strong>Calibrate</strong><span>Does confidence match performance?</span></div>
</div>

A cue that merely sounds “fuzzy” has not yet passed a task.
