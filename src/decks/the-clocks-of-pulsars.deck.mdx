# The clocks of pulsars

<p className="lede">A pulse train sounds simple. The journey from rotating neutron star to folded profile is not.</p>

---

## One object, three clocks

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="panel gold"><p className="panel-title">Rotation</p><p>A neutron star turns, sweeping a beam across space.</p></div>
  <div className="panel"><p className="panel-title">Arrival</p><p>A telescope timestamps energy received at Earth.</p></div>
  <div className="panel warm"><p className="panel-title">Playback</p><p>A designer chooses audio speed, grouping and emphasis.</p></div>
</div>

Only the first two clocks belong to the astronomical measurement. The third belongs to us.

## Interstellar space delays radio pulses

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Frequency channel</th><th>Relative arrival</th><th>Before folding</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>high radio frequency</td><td>earlier</td><td>align to reference time</td></tr>
    <tr><td>middle channel</td><td>later</td><td>remove predicted delay</td></tr>

This frequency-dependent delay is **dispersion**. Dedispersion is a scientific operation, not audio polishing.

---

## Folding reveals the profile

<div className="process-flow">
  <div><strong>Guess period</strong><span>Choose a trial rotation length.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Cut</strong><span>Divide the time series into rotations.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Align</strong><span>Place equal phase positions together.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Average</strong><span>Strengthen the repeated profile.</span></div>
</div>

Folding reveals repetition by discarding the identity of individual rotations.

## What folding removes

<div className="deck-grid">
  <div className="panel warm"><p className="panel-title">Hidden by averaging</p><ul><li>pulse-to-pulse variation</li><li>rare bright pulses</li><li>short interference</li></ul></div>
  <div className="panel gold"><p className="panel-title">Made visible</p><ul><li>stable phase structure</li><li>weak repeated components</li><li>profile comparison</li></ul></div>
</div>

The operation is valuable because it changes the question.

---

## Timing residuals are the next signal

Observed arrival time − predicted arrival time = **residual**.

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="metric gold"><strong>0</strong><span>model and observation agree</span></div>
  <div className="metric"><strong>trend</strong><span>period or position model may be incomplete</span></div>
  <div className="metric warm"><strong>jump</strong><span>event, clock issue or data-quality problem</span></div>
</div>

Sonifying residuals shifts attention from pulse rhythm to model error.
