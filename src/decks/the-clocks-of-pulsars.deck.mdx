# The clocks of pulsars

## Regular does not mean metronomic

SLOP3255 · Week 4

---

## What arrives at the telescope?

A rotating neutron star can sweep radio emission across Earth.

The telescope records radio intensity over time.

The series also contains receiver noise, background emission and propagation
effects.

The pulse is inferred from repeated structure.

---

## Folding reveals the profile

Choose a candidate period.

Divide the time series into equal periods.

Align and combine corresponding phases.

The result is a pulse profile built from many rotations — not one pristine
pulse.

---

## Two honest renderings

**Audify timing**  
Compress the time axis and preserve relative arrival intervals.

**Trigger events**  
Play one cue when a pulse crosses a stated threshold.

A drum grid or harmony can be added, but then it belongs to the designer.

---

{/* _class: compact */}

## What folding removes

Averaging strengthens the repeated profile.

It can suppress:

- pulse-to-pulse variation
- transient interference
- timing irregularity
- uncertainty about weak pulses

Clarity always has a cost.
