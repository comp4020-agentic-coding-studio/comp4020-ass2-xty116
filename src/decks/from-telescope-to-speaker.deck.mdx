# From telescope to speaker

## Every arrow is a decision

SLOP3255 · Week 2

---

{/* _class: compact */}

## A telescope does not hand us meaning

An instrument produces counts, voltages, arrival times or pixels.

Those values depend on:

- detector response
- exposure and sampling
- calibration references
- observing conditions

The “data” already has a history.

---

{/* _class: compact */}

## Reduction makes a claim possible

Raw detector images

→ subtract bias and dark signal  
→ apply flat-field correction  
→ measure a target against references  
→ attach time and units  
→ produce a light curve

Reduction is necessary. It is not invisible.

---

{/* _class: compact */}

## Then presentation begins

Scientific preparation and auditory presentation are separate layers.

**Preparation:** calibration, quality flags, derived quantities  
**Presentation:** window selection, normalisation, mapping, playback speed

Both must be documented, but they answer different questions.

---

{/* _class: compact */}

## A minimum provenance record

- source institution and dataset identifier
- retrieval date and licence
- original and derived units
- removed, filled or flagged values
- scale and range of every sound mapping
- software and version

Another person should be able to retrace the route.
