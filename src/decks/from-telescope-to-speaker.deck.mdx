# From telescope to speaker

<p className="lede">A sonification is only as trustworthy as the chain of decisions behind it.</p>

---

## A telescope does not hand us meaning

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="metric gold"><strong>counts</strong><span>detector response before physical calibration</span></div>
  <div className="metric"><strong>time</strong><span>exposure, cadence, gaps and clock standard</span></div>
  <div className="metric warm"><strong>context</strong><span>weather, pointing, comparison source and quality flags</span></div>
</div>

The “raw data” already belongs to an instrument, a place and a measurement protocol.

---

## The complete route

<div className="process-flow">
  <div><strong>Detect</strong><span>Photons become charge, counts or voltage.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Calibrate</strong><span>Correct known detector and observing effects.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Reduce</strong><span>Derive the quantity the scientific claim needs.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Present</strong><span>Select, scale and map the result to sound.</span></div>
</div>

Each stage changes the object. Provenance is the record of those changes.

---

{/* _class: compact */}

## Calibration is not cosmetic

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Correction</th><th>Problem addressed</th><th>If omitted</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Bias / dark</td><td>electronic offset and thermal signal</td><td>baseline drifts upward</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Flat field</td><td>uneven pixel sensitivity</td><td>position looks like brightness</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Reference star</td><td>changing atmosphere</td><td>cloud can resemble a transit</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Time standard</td><td>clock and location differences</td><td>events cannot be aligned</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

Calibration makes comparison possible; it does not make the result neutral.

---

## Preparation and presentation are different layers

<div className="deck-grid">
  <div className="panel gold"><p className="panel-title">Scientific preparation</p><ul><li>calibration</li><li>quality flags</li><li>background subtraction</li><li>derived quantities</li></ul></div>
  <div className="panel"><p className="panel-title">Auditory presentation</p><ul><li>window selection</li><li>normalisation</li><li>mapping range</li><li>playback speed</li></ul></div>
</div>

Document both layers, because they answer different questions.

---

## A minimum provenance record

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Field</th><th>Example entry</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Source</td><td>institution + dataset identifier + retrieval date</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Selection</td><td>object, channel, time interval, exclusions</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Operations</td><td>calibration, filtering, filling, normalisation</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Mapping</td><td>input units and output range for every sound dimension</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Software</td><td>tool, version and reproducible parameters</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

Another person should be able to retrace the route without guessing.

---
