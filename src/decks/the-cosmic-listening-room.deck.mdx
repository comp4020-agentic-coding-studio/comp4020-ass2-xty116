# The cosmic listening room

<p className="lede">The final instrument meets an audience who did not watch it being built. Can the evidence still travel?</p>

---

## The semester becomes one method

<div className="process-flow">
  <div><strong>Observe</strong><span>Source, units, sampling and limits.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Map</strong><span>Explicit auditory relationships.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Listen</strong><span>Task-based testing across channels.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Disclose</strong><span>Reconstructable pipeline and bounded claim.</span></div>
</div>

Week 12 is not a new technique. It is the public test of all four stages together.

---

## Anatomy of a listening station

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="panel gold"><p className="panel-title">Invitation</p><p>one question and a clear starting action</p></div>
  <div className="panel"><p className="panel-title">Instrument</p><p>playback, mapping and inspectable data</p></div>
  <div className="panel warm"><p className="panel-title">Evidence</p><p>source, operations, uncertainty and alternatives</p></div>
</div>

The visitor should not need the presenter beside them to use or question the work.

---

## First thirty seconds

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Visitor needs</th><th>Interface response</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>What is this?</td><td>one-sentence evidence claim</td></tr>
    <tr><td>What can I do?</td><td>one dominant play / explore action</td></tr>
    <tr><td>What am I hearing?</td><td>visible mapping and live status</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Can I stop?</td><td>persistent, immediate transport</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

Do not begin with a wall of instructions. Reveal depth as the visitor asks for it.

---

## Observe, do not pitch

<div className="deck-grid">
  <div className="choice warm"><p className="panel-title">Pitching</p><p>“Did you like it?”</p><span>Rewards politeness and aesthetic reaction.</span></div>
  <div className="choice gold"><p className="panel-title">Observing</p><p>“Show me where the main event occurred.”</p><span>Reveals whether the instrument supports its task.</span></div>
</div>

Watch hesitation, replay, accidental assumptions and recovery—not only verbal feedback.

---

## A public test protocol

<div className="process-flow">
  <div><strong>Consent</strong><span>Explain the test and recording boundary.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Prompt</strong><span>Give a scientific task, not instructions.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Observe</strong><span>Note action, timing and interpretation.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Debrief</strong><span>Reveal the mapping and invite correction.</span></div>
</div>

The visitor is not being graded. The instrument is.

---

## Readiness across four routes

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="panel gold"><p className="panel-title">Audio</p><p>safe level, immediate stop, meaningful cues</p></div>
  <div className="panel"><p className="panel-title">Visual</p><p>stable layout, labels, contrast, live state</p></div>
  <div className="panel warm"><p className="panel-title">Text + data</p><p>claim, values, provenance, uncertainty</p></div>
</div>

Then test keyboard-only, mobile width and reduced motion. Public means more than “deployed.”

## The opening-night checklist

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Evidence</th><th>Experience</th><th>Recovery</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>source is named</td><td>first action is clear</td><td>stop always works</td></tr>
    <tr><td>mapping is reconstructable</td><td>status is visible</td><td>errors explain next step</td></tr>
    <tr><td>uncertainty survives</td><td>no autoplay</td><td>alternative route exists</td></tr>
    <tr><td>claim is bounded</td><td>mobile fits</td><td>data remain inspectable</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

An opening is a testable condition, not a mood.

---

## The final claim card

<blockquote>
This instrument maps **[measured quantity]** from **[source]** to **[sound dimensions]** so a listener can **[task]**. It changes **[operations]**, preserves **[evidence]**, and cannot establish **[limit]**.
</blockquote>

If that sentence is vague, the public experience will be vague too.
