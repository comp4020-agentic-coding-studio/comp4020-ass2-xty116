# The cosmic listening room

## The instrument now meets its audience

SLOP3255 · Week 12

---

{/* _class: compact */}

## The public condition

A first-time visitor arrives without the designer.

They must be able to:

- understand the question
- begin and stop safely
- inspect the source and mapping
- use a non-audio route
- recognise the limits of the claim

Independence is the final test.

---

## Anatomy of a station

**Question** — one precise pattern to investigate  
**Instrument** — one excellent primary interaction  
**Disclosure** — source, processing, mapping, uncertainty  
**Alternative** — table, visual and structured description  
**Prompt** — one answerable listening task

Everything else is optional.

---

## Observe, do not pitch

Watch where a visitor:

- hesitates
- misreads a control
- confuses music with evidence
- misses uncertainty
- reaches the intended observation

Do not teach around the interface while testing it.

---

## Readiness check

The public URL works without login.

Playback never starts itself.

Keyboard and stop controls work.

The source values and mapping are visible.

At least five first-time users have attempted the same task.
