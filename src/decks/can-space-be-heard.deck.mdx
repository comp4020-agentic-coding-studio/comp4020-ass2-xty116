# Can space be heard?

## Three different claims hide inside one question

SLOP3255 · Week 1

---

## Sound needs a medium

Pressure waves travel through matter.

Most of the space between astronomical objects cannot carry sound to a
microphone.

Telescopes usually collect electromagnetic radiation, particles or changes in
an instrument — not an acoustic performance.

---

{/* _class: compact */}

## Three ways data reaches a speaker

**Recording**  
A microphone measures pressure over time.

**Audification**  
A measured time series is replayed, often shifted into hearing range.

**Parameter mapping**  
Data dimensions are assigned to pitch, time, level, timbre or position.

These are different evidence claims.

---

## One curve, two hearings

The source values stay fixed.

**Pitch mapping:** brightness → 220–660 Hz  
**Rhythm mapping:** brightness → interval length

If the interpretation changes, the mapping is doing intellectual work.

---

{/* _class: dense */}

## The accountable sentence

Do not write:

> This is the sound of an exoplanet.

Write:

> Relative brightness samples were mapped linearly to pitch and played at a
> fixed interval.

The second sentence can be checked.
