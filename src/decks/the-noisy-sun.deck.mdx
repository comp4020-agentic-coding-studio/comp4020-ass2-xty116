# The noisy Sun

## Signal, interference, or not enough evidence?

SLOP3255 · Week 5

---

## A dynamic spectrum has three variables

Horizontal position: time

Vertical position: radio frequency

Pixel intensity or colour: measured power

A sonification must turn this two-dimensional field into a temporal experience.

---

## The scan path writes the timeline

Left to right preserves observation time.

Bottom to top compares frequency at one moment.

A raster scan traverses every cell but may take too long.

The scan path is not part of the Sun. It is an interface decision.

---

{/* _class: compact */}

## Not every bright feature is solar

Ground-based radio data may contain:

- narrow terrestrial transmitters
- broadband interference
- receiver drift
- unavailable channels
- calibration artefacts

Unknown remains a valid label.

---

{/* _class: compact */}

## Silence can mislead

Removing a flagged cell may sound like a measured zero.

Better options include:

- a separate noise cue
- spoken quality flags
- an explicit gap
- parallel clean and unclean versions

The listener needs to know what was absent and what was removed.
