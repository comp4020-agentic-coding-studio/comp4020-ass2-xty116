# The noisy Sun

<p className="lede">A bright streak can be a solar burst, local interference, an instrument effect—or not enough evidence.</p>

---

## A dynamic spectrum has three variables

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="metric gold"><strong>x</strong><span>observation time</span></div>
  <div className="metric"><strong>y</strong><span>radio frequency channel</span></div>
  <div className="metric warm"><strong>colour</strong><span>received power or intensity</span></div>
</div>

Audio unfolds in one time dimension. We must decide how the second axis enters the listening experience.

## Different shapes imply different questions

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Visible pattern</th><th>Possible reading</th><th>Auditory test</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>fast frequency drift</td><td>changing emission region</td><td>does pitch sweep over time?</td></tr>
    <tr><td>vertical broadband line</td><td>short event or impulsive RFI</td><td>does it appear across channels together?</td></tr>
    <tr><td>persistent narrow band</td><td>local transmitter candidate</td><td>does one pitch remain fixed?</td></tr>
    <tr><td>blank rectangle</td><td>missing data or receiver state</td><td>is absence explicitly marked?</td></tr>
  </tbody>

Pattern recognition generates hypotheses. Metadata and comparison test them.

---

## Not every bright feature is solar

<div className="deck-grid">
  <div className="panel warm"><p className="panel-title">Radio-frequency interference</p><ul><li>may remain at fixed local frequencies</li><li>may begin or end abruptly</li><li>can overwhelm astrophysical structure</li></ul></div>
  <div className="panel gold"><p className="panel-title">Solar candidate</p><ul><li>compares across observatories</li><li>fits plausible drift behaviour</li><li>survives instrument-quality checks</li></ul></div>
</div>

The sonification should not turn a classification guess into certainty.

---

## Three routes through the same matrix

<div className="process-flow">
  <div><strong>Rows</strong><span>Hear one frequency channel over time.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Columns</strong><span>Hear the spectrum at each time step.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Peaks</strong><span>Track only strongest energy by time.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Layers</strong><span>Assign bands to distinct timbres.</span></div>
</div>

Each route discards structure. The legend must say which structure survives.

---

## Silence can mislead

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="choice gold"><p className="panel-title">Zero</p><p>The instrument measured no signal above its defined baseline.</p></div>
  <div className="choice warm"><p className="panel-title">Missing</p><p>No valid measurement exists for this interval.</p></div>
  <div className="choice"><p className="panel-title">Muted</p><p>The designer chose not to render a valid value.</p></div>
</div>

These states may all sound quiet, but they make different claims.

---

## Make the quality channel audible

<table className="mapping-table">

Quality should not compete with the main scientific pattern, but it cannot disappear.
