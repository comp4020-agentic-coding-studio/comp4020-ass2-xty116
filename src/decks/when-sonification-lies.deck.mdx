# When sonification lies

<p className="lede">A persuasive sound can make a weak claim feel certain. Technical accuracy alone does not remove rhetoric.</p>

---

## Every mapping carries associations

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="panel gold"><p className="panel-title">Rising pitch</p><p>may suggest growth, progress or approach</p></div>
  <div className="panel"><p className="panel-title">Regular rhythm</p><p>may imply stable sampling or periodic cause</p></div>
  <div className="panel warm"><p className="panel-title">Reverberation</p><p>may imply physical scale or distant space</p></div>
</div>

These meanings are culturally and musically available. They are not measured values.

---

## Four quiet ways to mislead

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Operation</th><th>What disappears</th><th>False impression</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Selection</td><td>ordinary intervals</td><td>dramatic event is typical</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Cleaning</td><td>ambiguity and noise</td><td>signal was obvious</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Quantisation</td><td>small variation</td><td>natural categories exist</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Decoration</td><td>boundary of measured data</td><td>music is evidence</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

The result may remain beautiful. The claim must remain bounded.

---

## The certainty amplifier

<div className="deck-grid">
  <div className="choice warm"><p className="panel-title">Source</p><p>Noisy values with overlapping uncertainty.</p><span>Several interpretations remain plausible.</span></div>
  <div className="choice gold"><p className="panel-title">Rendering</p><p>Quantised notes, strong cadence and triumphant resolution.</p><span>The ending feels more certain than the evidence.</span></div>
</div>

Emotional clarity can exceed evidential clarity.

---

## Run the hostile reading

Before explaining your work, ask a listener:

1. What do you think was measured?
2. What changed in the source?
3. Which sound features seem physically meaningful?
4. How certain does the conclusion feel?

Compare their inference with your actual pipeline. The mismatch is design evidence.

---

## Separate three layers

<div className="deck-grid three">
  <div className="metric gold"><strong>D</strong><span>measured data</span></div>
  <div className="metric"><strong>M</strong><span>mapping and processing</span></div>
  <div className="metric warm"><strong>C</strong><span>composition and decoration</span></div>
</div>

All three may be present. The ethical failure is letting C masquerade as D.

## The disclosure checklist

<table className="mapping-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Evidence</th><th>Design</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>source, object, retrieval date</td><td>scale, range and timing</td></tr>
    <tr><td>units and sampling</td><td>filtering and compression</td></tr>
    <tr><td>cleaning and missing values</td><td>musical additions and reverb</td></tr>
    <tr><td>uncertainty treatment</td><td>accessible alternatives</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

Disclosure makes critique possible; it does not excuse a manipulative mapping.

---

## Revise the strongest implication first

<div className="process-flow">
  <div><strong>Find</strong><span>Locate the largest listener inference.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Compare</strong><span>Does the source support that strength?</span></div>
  <div><strong>Reduce</strong><span>Remove or relabel the persuasive cue.</span></div>
  <div><strong>Retest</strong><span>Ask the hostile questions again.</span></div>
</div>

Ethics becomes an observable revision process, not a paragraph at the end.
