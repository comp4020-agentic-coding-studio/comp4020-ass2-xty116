# Policies and support

## Hearing safety

Playback never needs to be loud. Start at the lowest system volume, raise it
only to a comfortable level and take a five-minute quiet break after 45 minutes
of headphone work. Do not normalise submissions to maximise loudness. Every
interactive work must open silently, include an immediate stop control and
avoid sudden level changes.

Use speakers, visual inspection or a text/table alternative whenever headphones
are uncomfortable. Tell the teaching team before a lab if tinnitus, hearing
sensitivity or another access need changes how you should participate.

## Accessible participation

Sound is never the only route to a scientific fact in this course. Provide
source values, a structured description and a visual or tabular representation.
Interactive controls must be keyboard operable. Respect reduced-motion settings
and give listeners control over playback, speed and repetition.

Contact Eli for an individual adjustment. You do not need to disclose a
diagnosis. Equivalent work can use non-audio representations while still
addressing mapping, uncertainty and interpretation.

## Data and source attribution

For every dataset record the publisher, object or event identifier, retrieval
URL, retrieval date, units, licence where available, and every cleaning or
derived field. Never describe a parameter-mapped sonification as an unprocessed
recording. Musical quantisation, filtering, interpolation, time compression and
added ambience must appear in the mapping disclosure.

## AI and assistance

Generative AI may assist with code, prose revision or exploration. It may not
invent measurements, listening-test participants, citations or results.
Submissions include a short assistance statement naming tools, significant
prompts or uses, and the parts checked by the student. You remain responsible
for running the work, verifying its claims and understanding every submitted
mapping.

## Deadlines and extensions

Submit by the time shown on the assessment page. A submission received within
24 hours without an approved extension loses 5 percentage points; each further
24-hour period loses another 5 points, up to five days. Work more than five
days late is not accepted without an approved extension.

Request extensions through Slop University's student portal before the
deadline when possible. Email Amara if illness or access circumstances make the
portal impractical. Technical trouble is evidence for an extension only when
reported promptly with the failing file or deployment log.

## Getting help

Bring mapping and prototype questions to the Listening Lab. Email Amara for
course or assessment questions and Eli for access or audio-production support.
Questions that affect the whole class are answered on the course forum without
identifying the student who asked.
