# How to Hear the Universe harness

## Course position

- Build one coherent course: astronomical data sonification, not a general astronomy survey and not a music-production course.
- Treat sonification as an accountable mapping from data to sound. Never call an output "the sound of space" without naming the source data, transformation and uncertainty.
- The semester moves from signal literacy to mapping, case studies, uncertainty, accessibility, critique and a final instrument. Every week must advance that arc.
- Write in a precise, curious and unsentimental voice. Avoid cosmic awe filler, generic claims about innovation, and content-shaped repetition.

## Content contracts

- Keep the assigned code digits `255`; this course is `SLOP3255`.
- Publish exactly twelve dated lectures and twelve dated Listening Labs, one pair per teaching week.
- Every lecture names a driving question and a distinct data object. Every lab names a concrete output students leave with.
- Every lecture also carries an observation guide with at least three observable learning outcomes, inspectable teaching values, a worked method, an explained checkpoint and a primary source. Label teaching models so they cannot be mistaken for observations.
- Assessments total 100% and build toward one documented, accessible cosmic listening instrument.
- At least one lecture links a real deck. Policies cover hearing safety, data attribution, accessibility and responsible use of AI.
- Use real scientific concepts and clearly marked fictional teaching scenarios. Do not invent research findings, data provenance or legal requirements.

## Experience contracts

- Preserve the fixed SlopU identity, palette, collection keys, generated API and deployment plumbing.
- The visual language is a listening station: measured waveforms, timelines, instrument readouts and observation logs. Avoid a generic dark starfield theme.
- Visual richness must clarify the course: prefer the semester atlas, signal comparisons and evidence ledgers over decorative telemetry or repeated cards.
- Audio never autoplays. Every sound control has pause/stop, keyboard access, a visible mapping legend and a non-audio representation.
- Pages must remain usable at 1920x1080 and 390x844, under reduced motion, and when resized mid-interaction.
- The Listening Lab may be playful, but it demonstrates one honest mapping at a time and exposes the underlying values.

## Workflow

- Add contract tests before bulk content. Keep the build green after each implementation commit.
- Before accepting a substantial pass, follow the marker's ten-minute route through the home page, non-adjacent weeks, an assessment, a deck and policies at both published viewports.
- Use structured content and the existing collection schemas; do not duplicate course facts across pages.
- Verify every deck and interactive control in a real browser. Automated checks guard contracts; listening and visual review guard judgement.
- Keep commits small enough that `PROCESS.md` can cite consequential decisions rather than one final dump.
